Privacy Policy

CLUTCH · Last updated November 16, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what information CLUTCH collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Stored on your device

Your progress (cards, coins, records, settings) is saved in your browser on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes it, unless you have an account.

2. Accounts (optional)

  • If you make an account, we store your email address and a copy of your game progress so you can play on any device.
  • Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase. We never see your password.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time: Me tab → Delete account.

3. Game statistics (analytics)

To understand how the game is played and fix bugs, CLUTCH records, under a random ID for your device:

  • when and how long you play (including each play session), which modes and buttons you use, and game events (for example "finished a season" or "opened a pack");
  • if you are signed in, which account the device belongs to (your account ID and email), so play time can be counted per account;
  • your progress (level, coins, cards, records) and your GM name;
  • basic device info: device type, whether it is installed to your home screen, language, time zone and screen size;
  • error messages, if something breaks.

This data is stored in Google Firebase and only the developer can read it. It is never sold.

4. Features that share information with other players

  • Daily Draft rankings: your daily score, shared anonymously (no name).
  • Play with friends: your GM name and room code are sent through public real-time servers (EMQX and HiveMQ) so your friends can see you.
  • Transfer Market: your GM name appears on cards you list or buy.
  • Invite a friend: your invite code is linked to your account so we can give rewards.

5. Ads

CLUTCH may show ads, including optional "watch an ad for a reward" ads. Ads are provided by Google, which may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We ask your age once. Players who say they are under 13 only receive non-personalised ads.

6. Children

CLUTCH is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as email addresses from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, use Delete account in the game or contact us, and we will remove it.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your account and cloud save: Me tab → Delete account.
  • Remove data stored on your device: clear the site's data in your browser settings.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and mention it in the game's "What's new".

Questions? Contact the developer through the contact details on CLUTCH's app store or website listing.
